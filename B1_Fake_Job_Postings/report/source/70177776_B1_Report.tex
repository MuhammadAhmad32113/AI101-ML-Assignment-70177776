\begin{abstract}
Online recruitment fraud exposes applicants to financial extortion and identity theft. In this paper, we develop a multimodal classification pipeline on the EMSCAD benchmark (17,880 postings). By integrating $n$-gram TF-IDF representations of five free-text fields with engineered missingness indicators and categorical metadata, our best model—Calibrated Linear SVM—attains a fraud F1-score of 0.8907 (PR-AUC 0.9555) on the held-out test split, outperforming single-modality structured predictors by +90.3\% relative F1 gain.
\end{abstract}

\begin{IEEEkeywords}
Fraud Detection, Multimodal Learning, Class Imbalance, Natural Language Processing, ColumnTransformer.

\section{Introduction}
Online employment fraud has proliferated with digital recruitment platforms. We analyze the EMSCAD dataset containing 17,880 records. Fusing structured metadata with free-text representations produces an optimal F1 of 0.8907 via Calibrated Linear SVM.

\section{Problem Statement}
Each job record is defined as $\mathbf{x} = (\mathbf{x}_{\text{struct}}, \mathbf{x}_{\text{text}})$. We map $\mathbf{x} \to y \in \{0, 1\}$, with severe class imbalance ($\sim$4.84\% fraud). A naive majority baseline achieves 95.15\% accuracy while catching zero scams. We evaluate via Precision, Recall, F1, and PR-AUC.

\section{Dataset Used}
We use the EMSCAD dataset (Kaggle: \texttt{shivamb/real-or-fake-fake-jobposting-prediction}) with 17,880 records and 17 raw features across text, tabular, and binary attributes.

\section{Methodology and Results}
Pipelines combine \texttt{ColumnTransformer} with sublinear TF-IDF (20,000 features). Models evaluated include Balanced Logistic Regression, Calibrated Linear SVM, Balanced Random Forest, and LightGBM. Decision thresholds $\tau^*$ were tuned on the validation set.

Linear SVM achieved peak test F1 of 0.8907 (Precision 0.9402, Recall 0.8462, PR-AUC 0.9555). In the ablation study, combining modalities boosted F1 from 0.4378 (structured only) to 0.8333 (combined).

\section{Conclusion}
Multimodal fusion dramatically enhances fraud detection accuracy under extreme imbalance.
