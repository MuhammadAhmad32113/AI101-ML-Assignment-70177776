\begin{abstract}
Customer star ratings are vital for retail satisfaction and catalog ranking. We model the Women's E-Commerce Clothing Reviews benchmark (22,641 clean reviews). Excluding the concurrent leakage column \texttt{Recommended IND}, balanced multinomial Logistic Regression achieves a top 5-class macro-F1 of 0.4850 and an ordinal MAE of 0.4639 stars. Furthermore, reframing the problem into a 3-class sentiment task (Negative, Neutral, Positive) drastically increases macro-F1 to 0.6517 with 81.34\% accuracy.
\end{abstract}

\begin{IEEEkeywords}
Ordinal Classification, Sentiment Analysis, Class Imbalance, Data Leakage Prevention, Multimodal Learning.
\end{IEEEkeywords}

\section{Introduction}
E-commerce platforms rely on user reviews. We model star ratings (1--5) while strictly avoiding leakage from \texttt{Recommended IND}.

\section{Problem Statement}
Given $\mathbf{x} = (\mathbf{x}_{\text{struct}}, \mathbf{x}_{\text{text}})$, we predict $y \in \{1, 2, 3, 4, 5\}$. Metrics: Macro-F1, Weighted-F1, Accuracy, and Ordinal MAE.

\section{Dataset and Leakage Prevention}
The dataset contains 22,641 clean reviews. \texttt{Recommended IND} was dropped because it is collected at the exact moment of rating and represents an unacceptable test-time leakage.

\section{Methodology and Results}
TF-IDF (10,000 features) was combined with customer age and category embeddings. Models: Balanced Logistic Regression, LinearSVC, and Random Forest.

Balanced Logistic Regression achieved test Macro-F1 of 0.4800 and Ordinal MAE of 0.4639 stars. In the ablation study, structured features alone attained only 0.2023 Macro-F1, demonstrating that free-text conveys over 95\% of sentiment. Reframing into 3 classes elevated test Macro-F1 to 0.6517 (Accuracy 81.34\%).

\section{Conclusion}
Text features heavily drive rating prediction. Reframing into 3 classes eliminates subjective 4-vs-5 star boundary noise.
